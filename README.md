# Rain Dodger

**Plan motorcycle routes that keep you dry.**

Rain Dodger is a native iOS app for motorcyclists that combines Apple Maps routing with live weather forecasting so you can see where — and *when* — rain will hit your ride before you leave.

## Why

Getting caught in rain on a bike isn't just uncomfortable, it's dangerous. Rain Dodger answers one question at a glance: **which route keeps me dry, and where will it still be raining when I get there?**

## Key Features

- **Interactive map** — Full-screen Apple Maps home screen with your position, one-thumb recenter, and search in one tap.
- **Destination search** — Find any place by name with live MapKit results; recents remembered for faster next rides.
- **Trip planner** — Google Maps–style sheet with origin, destination, an optional stop, and a "Leave at" departure time. Get 2–3 route alternatives with ETA and distance.
- **Rain forecast along the route** — Every 5 km of your route is sampled for precipitation probability at your estimated arrival time, then color-coded on the map:
  - 🔵 Blue — under 30% chance of rain
  - 🟡 Yellow — 30–60%
  - 🔴 Red — 60% or more
- **Arrival-time badges** — Wet stretches show the time you'll arrive there, so you know exactly when you'll ride into rain.
- **Route detail preview** — Turn-by-turn breakdown of the selected route with rain chance per step and a "Rain on X km of Y km" summary.
- **Rider-first navigation aids** — Gyro-driven head arrow, custom compass, and a heading-lock button that keeps the map oriented the way you're facing while mounted.
